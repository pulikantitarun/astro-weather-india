# Non-cellular V5.2 assembly

The supported printable set is `stl_unified_v5/`. Parts are intentionally
unmarked; identify them by filename.

1. Print and approve `L5_Fit_Kit_12_PIECES_PRINT_FIRST.stl`.
2. Secure the ESP32 and auxiliary board to O5.
3. Press O5 evenly onto the four A5 posts and confirm every retaining head is
   visible above the carrier.
4. Complete and bench-test the low-voltage wiring.
5. Latch B5 onto A5, then press A5 into the front-left K5 dock.
6. Assemble the radiation shield and click it into the circular dock.
7. Install the sky sensors in I5, route their loom through the rim notch, then
   lower H5 vertically over all four split pins. Press evenly at the four
   corners until every head appears above H5; do not twist the optical towers.
8. Install the rain board in J5 and press the sloped cradle into its four clips.
9. Route the looms below all six rear cable jaws with service and drip loops.
10. Add redundant UV-resistant safety ties before outdoor use.

See [`UNIFIED_V5.md`](UNIFIED_V5.md) for print settings, removal instructions,
validation details and physical-test limits.
